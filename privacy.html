Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026. RANPA EXIM PVT LTD (“Ranpa Exim”, “we”, “us”) operates ranpaeximagri.com. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details. When you use the contact form, you provide your name, email address and message. On submit, the enquiry opens in WhatsApp (a Meta service) addressed to us, so the message is sent through WhatsApp. We do not store form submissions on this website's server.
  • Usage analytics. We use SimpleAnalytics and Google Analytics to see aggregated visits (pages viewed, approximate region, device type). These tools may set cookies or use IP-based processing as described in their own policies.
  • Visitor counter. A third-party hit counter records page loads to display a visitor count.

Cookies

This site uses cookies or similar storage for analytics, Google Fonts, Google Translate, and embedded content (for example, the Google Maps embed on the Contact page). You can block cookies in your browser; the site will still work, but some embeds may not load.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your export enquiries and provide quotations.
  • To improve the website and understand which products visitors are interested in.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

Enquiries you send go to us through WhatsApp. Analytics data is processed by our analytics providers. We do not share enquiry details with third parties for marketing.

Retention

Enquiry messages are kept in our business correspondence for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and for normal business records. Analytics data is retained per the analytics provider's settings.

Your rights

You may ask us to correct or delete your enquiry details. Contact us at the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
