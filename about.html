About Us

Who We Are ❓

At Ranpa Foods, we are a global leader in providing premium, sustainable food products. With decades of expertise in sourcing and delivering high-quality organic produce, we are committed to excellence, ensuring every product meets rigorous standards of freshness and sustainability. Our passion for quality drives us to connect farmers, suppliers, and consumers worldwide.

Our Vision

Our vision is to revolutionize global food supply chains by fostering sustainable practices and delivering unparalleled quality to every corner of the world. We aim to be the trusted name in organic and eco-friendly food products, inspiring healthier communities and a thriving planet. Through innovation, transparency, and collaboration with farmers and partners worldwide.

Our Mission

To achieve our vision, we partner with local farmers and global suppliers to ensure ethical sourcing and sustainable production. We leverage advanced logistics and innovative technologies to deliver fresh, high-quality products while minimizing our environmental footprint. Our commitment is to empower communities, promote health, and preserve the planet for future generations.

👨‍💼 Founder Profile

JCI Sen. Kranthi Kumar R

JCI Senator Kranthi Kumar R is a visionary entrepreneur and global business networker committed to promoting Indian excellence in international trade. As the Founder and Managing Director of RANPA EXIM, he has built a reputed brand known for reliability, noble performance, and quality in agricultural exports.

He has successfully completed professional training in Import–Export Management from iiiEM (International Institute of Import and Export Management), gaining strong expertise in export operations, documentation, and global trade compliance. His leadership has enabled RANPA EXIM to connect Indian farmers with global buyers through sustainable and transparent business practices.

Recognized as a JCI Senator for exceptional leadership and service through Junior Chamber International (JCI), he has represented India at several global trade and leadership forums. A passionate social contributor, he actively engages in youth empowerment and community development initiatives.

Driven by trust and integrity, he believes in building long-term business relationships that create lasting value for partners, clients, and communities worldwide.
